About Me

I am a Richard and Loan Hill Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at UIC, which I joined in January 2009. My background includes a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from McGill University and a Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences from Harvard University.

My research interests are in multi-user information theory and its applications to cognitive and software-defined radio, radar, massive multiple access, low-latency communication, two-way communication, and wireless networks. More recently, I have also been working on statistical and information-theoretic analysis of hardware security systems and interpretable learning for error-correcting codes.

Research

  • Information theory for wireless, relay, interference, and feedback channels
  • Joint sensing and communication, including communication in radar environments
  • Interpreting deep-learned error-correcting codes
  • Statistical analysis of hardware security and physically unclonable functions
